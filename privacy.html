Nuo Personal

Privacy information and use

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

Nuo is a private assistant operated by Daulet for his own use. This page describes its Google connection.

Access and purpose

Depending on the connected account, read-only permissions cover Gmail messages, calendars the account can access, saved contacts and Other Contacts, and account email identity. They support answering the owner's requests about email, schedules and people. Current assistant tools expose calendar event reads, Gmail searches, message reads and thread reads. They expose no Google write tools or contact tools.

Processing and sharing

The assistant and its gog integration run on the owner's Mac. Selected Google content may be sent to the configured AI provider to answer a request, and answers may be delivered to the owner's Telegram chat. Local processing does not mean that all data remains exclusively on the Mac. AI-provider and Telegram processing is subject to those services' policies and the owner's account settings. Nuo itself does not sell Google data or operate an advertising or model-training service.

Credentials

OAuth credentials are stored using macOS Keychain. They are not included in this website or passed to the language model as message content.

Retention

Retrieved excerpts and answers may remain in local conversation history, logs and backups. There is currently no single automatic deletion deadline covering every copy. Removing a memory or revoking Google access does not automatically erase old transcripts or backup copies.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke access through Google Account connections, disable the local integration, and separately remove retained local history and backups. Revocation prevents further authorized reads but does not remove previously retained copies.

Scope and use

This is a private personal-use integration with no public signup. Accounts are connected only with their owner's authorization and subject to any applicable organization policies. The Google connection does not authorize sending email, creating drafts, changing events or contacts, or deleting Google data. Any later expansion needs new configuration and authorization, and this page must be updated to reflect it.

Contact

Use the developer support email displayed on Nuo's Google consent screen.
