Nuo Personal

Nuo is Daulet's private personal assistant. It helps its owner review calendars and find information in email through a private Telegram conversation. There is no public signup.

Google access

The Google connection uses read-only permissions. Depending on the connected account, these cover Gmail, calendars, contacts (including automatically saved Other Contacts), and account email identity. It does not request an organization directory. Current assistant tools support Gmail and calendar reads; contact access is not exposed through the current tools.

The Google connection cannot send email, create drafts, change calendar events or contacts, or delete Google data.

The assistant and Google integration run on the owner's Mac. Selected Google content may be sent to the configured AI provider to answer a request, and answers may be delivered through Telegram.

Privacy information and use

Access can be revoked at any time through Google Account connections.

For questions, contact the developer support email displayed on Nuo's Google consent screen.

This website contains public information about the connection. It does not provide a login or access to the assistant.
